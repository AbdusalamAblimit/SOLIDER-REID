### 结论

1. **3-view parallel aug 是最强的单一训练技术**: +1.1/+1.7 vs exp166 (1-view CE)
2. **3-view+CE ≈ 1-view+SupCon**: exp190 (64.2/75.6) vs exp176 (64.1/75.5)，几乎完全一致
3. **SupCon 在 3-view 下仍有额外 +0.7/+1.0**: exp187 (64.9/76.6) vs exp190
4. **3-view > OA-SD**: exp190 (+1.1/+1.7) vs exp191 (+2.9/+2.6 from CE base, but lower abs)
5. **exp193 (3-view + OA-SD + CE) 是合理的下一步**
